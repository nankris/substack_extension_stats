                    Privacy

                    Quire stores everything locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local. Nothing you subscribe to, favorite, or save is sent to any external server. When live data retrieval runs, it only reads what's already visible on Substack pages you have open — Quire never asks for or handles your Substack password.
